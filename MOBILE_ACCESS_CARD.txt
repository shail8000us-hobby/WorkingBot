┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🔧 SERVICE MANAGEMENT                                            │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Check Status:
    lsof -i :5556 | grep LISTEN

  Restart Mobile API:
    kill $(lsof -t -i:5556)
    cd webui/backend
    python3 -c "import subprocess, sys; proc = subprocess.Popen([sys.executable, 'mobile_api.py'], stdout=open('mobile_api.log', 'w'), stderr=subprocess.STDOUT, start_new_session=True); print(f'Started PID: {proc.pid}')"

  View Logs:
    tail -f webui/backend/mobile_api.log
